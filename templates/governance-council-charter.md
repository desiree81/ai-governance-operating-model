# AI Governance Council Charter (Outline)
A template. Adapt names and bodies to your organization. The point of the charter is decision rights: everyone should know what this council can approve, what it can stop, and where an appeal goes.

## Purpose
The council owns the organization's AI risk posture. It approves high-risk AI systems, sets governance policy and tiering standards, and reviews the enterprise AI inventory on a set cadence.

## Membership
- Chair: the executive accountable for AI governance.
- Voting members: technology, data, security, privacy, legal, risk, and the business units that own AI systems.
- Advisory, non-voting: architecture, audit liaison, ethics or responsible AI lead.
- Quorum: chair plus a majority of voting members, including security and privacy for any Tier 4 decision.

## Decision rights
- Approve, condition, or reject Tier 3 and Tier 4 systems at each lifecycle gate.
- Grant time-boxed exceptions to governance policy, recorded in the inventory with an expiry date.
- Order a system paused or retired when monitoring or incident evidence warrants it.
- Set and amend the risk tiering standard and intake requirements.

## What the council does not do
- It does not design or build systems. Owners build; the council governs.
- It does not review Tier 1 or Tier 2 systems individually. It reviews their aggregate picture through inventory reporting.

## Cadence and inputs
- Meets on a fixed cadence (monthly is typical) and on call for Tier 4 incidents.
- Standing inputs: inventory changes since last session, systems due or overdue for re-review, monitoring exceptions, incidents, and exception requests.
- Decisions, conditions, and dissent are minuted and attached to the system's inventory record.

## Escalation
- An owner who disagrees with a decision may escalate once, to the executive sponsor named in the intake record, with the council's reasoning attached.
- A system that cannot meet a condition by its deadline returns to the council before the deadline, not after.

## Success measures
- Percentage of AI systems in the inventory with a named owner and a current review.
- Median time from intake to decision, by tier (low tiers should be fast).
- Overdue re-reviews trending to zero.
- Exceptions granted, and how many expire on time versus quietly renew.
